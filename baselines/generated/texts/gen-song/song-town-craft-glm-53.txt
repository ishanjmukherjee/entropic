Daddy's Ford still cold in the drive at five,
Mama left the porch light burning all night,
I packed my boots in with the good guitar,
Left the bad one hanging at the Rusty Barrel bar.

So fill it up, Regular, and point me toward the four-lane,
Miss Dot's Waffle House is the last thing I'll see,
If I never make it back, tell 'em I was just chasing daylight,
Not running from a thing, just heading where a thing can find me.

Junior pulled his rig up next to pump two,
Said, "Your granddad stacked hay where they built the bank."
We sat on the tailgate splitting an apple in half,
Like we were ten again, like he wasn't asking me to stay.

So fill it up, Regular, and point me toward the four-lane,
Radio's got nothing but static off the county line,
If I never make it back, tell 'em I was just chasing daylight,
Some folks plant, some folks just keep the road in mind.

Ten miles out, passed the Hardee's torn down,
Where Kayla Kessler said she'd wait for me in June.
The corn's still standing where she never did,
And the billboard says Memphis is two hundred miles too soon.

So fill it up, Regular — I say it to a stranger,
Somebody's pumping diesel where the old dog used to lay,
If I never make it back, tell 'em I was just chasing daylight,
And tell Dot to kill the porch light, somebody's on their way.
