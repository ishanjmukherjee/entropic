At half past four on Sycamore
Dad backed the Ford out to the street
I laid my lunch pail on the floor
My tool chest took the shotgun seat
He tore the bottom off the bill
For two new tires at Sonny's Shell
And drew the turns to I-35
On the back of what I owed him still

Keep the water tower on your right
Till the road bends out of town

Till the cornfields cut it down

I took a room above a bar
At six I punched a time clock in
And welded steps for railroad cars
Each Friday I sent twenty home
Till Dad mailed back a single dime
Taped heads up where he'd drawn the turn
That got me onto 35

This June my girl drove Grandpa home
From Sunday lunch at Sonny's Shell
She set his walker by the door
He slipped her something folded well
Back in the Ford she smoothed it flat
The tape was brown, the dime still shone
She laid it heads up on the dash
And took the wheel to get us home
