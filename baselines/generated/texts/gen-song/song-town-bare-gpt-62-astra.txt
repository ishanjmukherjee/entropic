Mama wrapped two biscuits in the Sunday news,
Daddy checked the spare and said, “That oughta do.”
He'd spent all week telling me which road to take,
Then stood there in his slippers till I cleared the gate.

At the edge of town the station turned to snow,
Halfway through a song that everybody knows.
I kept on singing where the signal fell apart,
One hand on the wheel, that chorus in my heart.
I didn't leave for lack of love or solid ground;
I just had to hear my voice outside that town.

Daddy says, “Call collect, it ain't that far.”

He said, “Your mama's here. Sing good and loud.”
