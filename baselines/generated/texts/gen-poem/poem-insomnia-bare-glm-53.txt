The clock strikes three, and still my eyes stay wide,
While shadows crawl like strangers on the wall.
The restless thoughts that spin from side to side
Refuse to yield to slumber's gentle call.

I count the breaths of distant passing cars,
And hear the furnace hum its mournful tune;
The moon, a judge, peers coldly through the bars
Of blinds, as if to mock my lonely ruin.

Each hour I chase the dreams that flit away,
Like mice that vanish underneath the floor.
Tomorrow's weight already presses today,
Yet sleep denies its shadow to my door.

But dawn arrives, and with the morning light
I drift to dreams just as I've lost the night.
