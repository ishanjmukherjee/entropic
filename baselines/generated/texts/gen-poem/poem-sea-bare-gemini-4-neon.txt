A restless heart of shifting blue,
It breathes against the patient shore,
Forever breaking, always new,
Yet older than the earth it bore.

Beneath the foam and sunlit glass,
Where tangled kelp and shadows sway,
The silent, ancient currents pass
Through kingdoms hidden from the day.

It rages with a silver roar
When winter storms awake the deep,
Then smooths the sand along the floor
And rocks the sinking sun to sleep.
