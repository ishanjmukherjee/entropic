The name I knew dissolves like morning frost,
Each syllable I chase slips through my hand;
So much is lost, and still so much is lost.

I walk the rooms where memory once crossed,
A stranger now in some familiar land;
The name I knew dissolves like morning frost.

Photographs grow yellow, faces ghosted,
Their voices fading into silver sand;
So much is lost, and still so much is lost.

I hold a key whose lock has long been tossed,
A letter signed by someone soft and unplanned;
The name I knew dissolves like morning frost.

Perhaps forgetting is a gentle cost
The mind bestows, a mercy kindly planned;
So much is lost, and still so much is lost.

At dusk I sit and mourn what I have lost,
Then wake to find I can't quite understand—
The name I knew dissolves like morning frost,
So much is lost, and still so much is lost.
