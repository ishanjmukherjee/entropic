This bed, a rack where weary limbs are stretched,
The ceiling's blank canvas begins to paint
With phantom worries, shadow-forms half-fetched,
The whispered verdict of a nameless saint.
I chase the tail of sleep around the room,
It flickers in the static of my mind,
A distant drumming prophesying doom
To which the ticking clock is deaf and blind.
The hours fall like soldiers in a war
No one remembers starting; dark, still dark
Becomes a texture never felt before,
A smothering and seamless, heavy ark.
At last, a grey and gritty light leaks through.
The world awakes to start the day anew.
