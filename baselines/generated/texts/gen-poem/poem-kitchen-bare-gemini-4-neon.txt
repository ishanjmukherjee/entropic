Morning light spills across the tile,
The kettle wakes with a silver sigh.
Dark coffee blooms in a ceramic mug,
While quiet shadows slowly die.

A wooden board bears the rhythm of knives,
Garlic and thyme in the sizzling pan.
Steam climbs up to the ceiling's edge,
Where the warmth of the evening first began.

Flour dust settles on apron strings,
The oven glows with a golden grace.
Stories are stirred into simmering pots,
And laughter lingers in this space.

At last, the dishes rest in rows,
The refrigerator hums its low refrain.
A quiet heart of the sleeping house,
Waiting to feed the day again.
