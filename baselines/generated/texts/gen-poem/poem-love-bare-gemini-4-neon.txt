You are the quiet hum of morning light,
The steady warmth that lingers in the room,
A gentle harbor in the deepest night,
A sudden garden breaking into bloom.

In every word, a melody I know,
In every touch, a language of our own,
You are the compass everywhere I go,
The truest home my wandering heart has known.

Let seasons turn and scatter all their gold,
Let years unfold like pages in the sun,
With you, my hand will never feel the cold,
Two separate lives now braided into one.
