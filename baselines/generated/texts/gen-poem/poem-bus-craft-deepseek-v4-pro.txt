Between 10th and Yamhill the doors fold shut
on a green-vested man and his bicycle frame
with the back wheel missing. Eleven twenty.
A woman in a burgundy smock
peels a hard-boiled egg into a napkin.
Her phone lights up her chin.

From the rear bench a transistor radio
plays a Royals game nobody turns off.
The bus kneels at the curb on Morrison
and a boy steps up with a pizza box
warm in his arms and sits
without opening the lid.

The driver calls out the bridge lift
before it appears, the steel deck
angled against the river.
A shopping bag slides two feet
when the floor tilts
and nobody reaches for it.

Under the halogen ceiling
a teenager uses a dry-erase marker
to draw a long-necked bird on the window.
The ink is green and smells of acetone.
It will wipe away with a sleeve.
The drawbridge grates back down into place.
