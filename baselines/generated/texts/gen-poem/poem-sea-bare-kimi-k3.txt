The tide writes whispers on the sand,
In silver scripts, then takes them back—
A million years of breath and sigh
Are stored inside each wave’s blue arc.

The gulls are specks against the glass
Of light that shatters on the crest,
While deep below, the darkness dreams
Of ships that sank and found their rest.

The salt is heavy on the lips,
The horizon bends to meet the sky,
And in the pause between the swells
We hear the earth’s eternal cry—

Not grief, not joy, but something older,
The pulse that moves the blood in veins,
The sea that cradles us and breaks us,
And gathers us back home again.
