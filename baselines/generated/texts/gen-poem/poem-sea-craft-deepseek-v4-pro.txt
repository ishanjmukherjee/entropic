The cartographer’s green lamp
sheds a cone of midges
on the western channel, five fathoms
off the Porcupine Bank.
A tern folds and drops.

The radio stays open on the trawler’s dash
but nobody calls.
Deckhand hooks a thumbnail
in the zip of his oilskin
and it parts
one tooth at a time.

At ten past three
the shift-haul begins.
Monkfish come over the rail
in their own black slime,
the size of feed sacks,
gaping.

A boy hoses the deck. He trains
the jet on a clot of scale
until the steel shows through.
Trough-deep, between the swell
and the hull, a boiler suit someone
weighted and sent down
opens its sleeves.

The swell keeps its counsel
beneath the bow-thrust,
lifts and does not break.
