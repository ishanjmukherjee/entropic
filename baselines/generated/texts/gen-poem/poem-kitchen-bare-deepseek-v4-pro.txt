Here is the vessel where the day begins,
a tiled republic of simmer and steam.
The copper kettle on its throne of flame
unlooses its first, ferocious, captive scream.

The window glass, a membrane to the dark,
now slowly images a brighter world:
the pot of basil on the sill awakes,
its small green catechism leaf-unfurled.

The knife discovers, with a certain grace,
the hidden architecture of a pear,
exposing, on the scarred and honest board,
a private constellation sleeping there.

A bowl contains a universe of dough,
a breathing moon of yeast and patient dust.
The radio, in hushed and crackling tones,
conspires with flour in a yeast-born trust.

The iron skillet holds ancestral heat,
a charred cosmology of flame and fat,
where onion apparitions softly weep
their vapored souls on this domestic flat.

And in the cupboard’s dark confessional,
the spices keep their mysteries contained:
the cinnamon’s dry heat, the clove’s dark pang,
a genesis of flavors, preordained.

All chaos is subdued to ritual here,
all hunger finds its answer or its shape.
The afternoon leaks honey from a spoon,
the evening is a star anise escape.

The day’s last act: the porcelain is stacked,
the surfaces are wiped of their desire.
The quiet furnace of the pilot light
bends its blue thought above a violet fire.
