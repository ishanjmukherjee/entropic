At eleven the 49 pulls up under the tracks
at Western and Lake. A man gets on
with a kitchen drawer against his chest.
He sets it on the fare box to find his card.

The driver says, Take your time.
A train goes over before the card takes.
The drawer has one blue knob
and a strip of flowered paper in the bottom.

At Madison a woman in clogs gets off.
The man slides into her seat.
A little boy across the aisle takes a potato
from the hood of his coat. His mother says,
I looked all over the store for that.

The bus brakes. A screw rolls the length
of the drawer and back. The man tips it
into his palm, then puts it in his shirt pocket.

At Roosevelt the cord comes down.
He stands with the drawer tucked under one arm,
four black tracks on his cuff
where the runners rubbed.
