The summer green begins to fade,
To amber, rust, and glowing gold,
A quiet chill invades the shade,
As evening winds grow sharp and cold.

The maples drop their crimson leaves,
To dance upon the frosted ground,
While through the bare and trembling trees,
A hollow, whistling breeze is found.

The scent of woodsmoke fills the air,
The daylight slips away too fast,
A gentle hush is everywhere,
As nature sleeps, the season's past.
