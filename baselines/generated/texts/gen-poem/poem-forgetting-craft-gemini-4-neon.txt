At four the lake-wind shifts across the bay.
A freighter backs out from the Duluth dock.
The brass key sits upon the metal tray.

A neighbor in the hallway asks the way
to Canal Park, then checks the lobby clock.
At four the lake-wind shifts across the bay

and drives the newsprint down the gravel. Gray
water slaps the pilings. Every lock
rejects the teeth; I set it on the tray.

My brother had a molar pulled in May,
or ten years back; the dentist wore a smock.
At four the wind comes cold across the bay.

A woman on the bus has much to say
about the price of oats, the cinderblock
garage. The brass key sits upon the tray.

The radiator clanks and spits its spray
against the plaster. Gulls drop on the rock.
At four the lake-wind shifts across the bay.
The brass key cools upon the metal tray.
