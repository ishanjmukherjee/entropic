I set a place for you again tonight.
The years have packed your letters all away.
Your name goes out of mind, but not the light.

I tilt the lamp until the cloth is white
And smooth a crease exactly as you’d say.
I set a place for you again tonight.

I try to get the little details right:
The blue glass and the roses on the tray.
Your name goes out of mind, but not the light.

The spoon beside your plate is worn and bright.
My thumb has rubbed the silver down to gray.
I set a place for you again tonight.

I had a word for this when I could write.
I find the page. The ink has run astray.
Your name goes out of mind, but not the light.

And if I lose the last of you by sight,
My hands will know the shape of your old way.
I set a place for you again tonight.
Your name goes out of mind, but not the light.
