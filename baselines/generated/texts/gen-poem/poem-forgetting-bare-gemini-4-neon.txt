The faces that I knew begin to fade,
Like chalk beneath a slow and steady rain,
I lose the quiet promises we made.

The sunlit afternoons where once we played
Dissolve into a dull and nameless pain;
The faces that I knew begin to fade.

I reach for echoes in the evening shade,
But only hollow silences remain;
I lose the quiet promises we made.

The photographs grow yellow and decayed,
I search the empty rooms of mind in vain;
The faces that I knew begin to fade.

I tell myself I will not be afraid,
Though every golden moment leaves no stain;
I lose the quiet promises we made.

So let the light go out, the debt be paid,
Let winter frost the glass and blur the pane;
The faces that I knew begin to fade,
I lose the quiet promises we made.
