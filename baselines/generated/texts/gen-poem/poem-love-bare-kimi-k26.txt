You are the quiet between heartbeats,
the pause before rain,
the single star I find first
when the others blur and wane.

I have loved you in languages
I never learned to speak,
in the tilt of morning light,
in the weight of the week.

Your name, a door I open
without knowing the room,
the dark familiar as skin,
the air honeyed with gloom.

I would fail at every metaphor
before I'd fail at this:
the plain, unbearable fact
of your wrist, of your wrist.
