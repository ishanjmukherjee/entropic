A rolling box of pale blue light,
it cuts the heavy velvet dark.
The air brakes hiss a weary sigh
beside the shadows of the park.

Inside, a scattered, silent crew—
a nurse in scrubs, a man asleep,
all cradled by the engine’s hum
through hours only night-shifts keep.

The windows turn to darkened glass,
reflecting tired, ghostly eyes,
while neon bleeds across the rain
beneath the ink-black city skies.

A yellow cord, a sudden chime,
the folding doors swing wide and close.
A glowing ship of quiet souls,
it sails the empty streets, and goes.
