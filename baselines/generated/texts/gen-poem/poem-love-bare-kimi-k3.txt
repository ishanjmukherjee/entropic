In the quiet before dawn,
when the world forgets its weight,
I find you in the steam of coffee,
in the creases of the bed we made,
in the way my name becomes
something sacred on your tongue.

You are the pause in my chaos,
the steady drum beneath the static,
the reason maps no longer frighten me—
for every road now leads
to where you are.

I have loved you in a thousand lives:
as watercolor bleeding into canvas,
as the space between stars
that somehow burns brighter than the light,
as the breath I didn’t know I was holding
until you exhaled.

Stay with me.
Not as an anchor,
but as the tide that teaches the shore
it is allowed to move.

Stay with me.
Not forever—
that is too small a word—
but for every moment
after.
