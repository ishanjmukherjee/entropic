I've lost the names I used to keep
in boxes labeled with such care—
what I forgot, I cannot keep.

The photographs begin to sleep
in albums faded by the air.
I've lost the names I used to keep.

The birthdays sink into the deep
of calendars I left somewhere.
What I forgot, I cannot keep.

The faces that I used to reap
from memory's field now disappear.
I've lost the names I used to keep.

The stories that I meant to steep
in telling them dissolve to spare.
What I forgot, I cannot keep.

Now even absence cannot keep
the shape of what was never there.
I've lost the names I used to keep;
what I forgot, I cannot keep.
