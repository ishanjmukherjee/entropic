The morning light spills gold across the counter,
where coffee steam rises like a whispered prayer.
The kettle hums its small familiar anthem,
and toast perfumes the stillness of the air.

Here flour dusts the wooden board like snowfall,
and onions weep their sharp and honest tears.
The window holds a slice of changing seasons—
spring blossoms, autumn leaves, the winter's hush.

The fridge door swings, a keeper of leftovers,
small archives of the meals we shared before.
Scratched pots and pans hang ready for tomorrow,
each spoon a witness to the years gone by.

At dusk, the stove light glows a quiet amber,
transforming chopping boards to altars of care.
For every kitchen is a heart made visible,
where love is measured out in cups and warmth.
