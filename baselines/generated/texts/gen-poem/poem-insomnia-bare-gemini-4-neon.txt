The world is hushed beneath a heavy shroud,
While shadows stretch across the bedroom wall,
Yet in my skull the racing thoughts are loud,
And answer to a silent, frantic call.
I twist and turn within the tangled sheet,
And watch the crimson numbers slowly creep,
My weary body begs for soft retreat,
Yet stubborn eyes refuse the gift of sleep.
Old ghosts of errors whisper in the dark,
Tomorrow's burdens weigh upon my chest,
Each fleeting worry strikes a sudden spark,
That burns away the quiet hope of rest.
At last the pale and bitter morning breaks,
To find me spent before the day awakes.
