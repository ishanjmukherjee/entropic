QUEEN MARGARET

(Blackout. A single candle. She enters. Removes her ring. Places it on the table. Begins again.)

Messenger. You have the floor.

MESSENGER

The floor, Your Majesty?

QUEEN MARGARET

The boards. The planks. Whatever sustains you.

MESSENGER

I was not—

QUEEN MARGARET

You were seen. Half an hour crossing the courtyard. The steward has a limp. You don't. Therefore you are new. Therefore you carry something.

(picks up the ring, does not look at it)

I prefer to stand for arrivals.

MESSENGER

The eastern road—

QUEEN MARGARET

Close.

MESSENGER

Your Majesty?

QUEEN MARGARET

From the east, yes. The eastern road is close. An autumn smell. Wet leather. I know the eastern road.

(slips the ring onto her smallest finger)

You will eat.

MESSENGER

I am not—

QUEEN MARGARET

The barley in this storeroom is eleven years stored. From before. A siege taste. You will tell me if it keeps.

(retrieves the bowl from the table. Stays near him)

The spoon is pewter. My grandmother's. She fed six children through a winter with that spoon.

MESSENGER

I cannot stay—

QUEEN MARGARET

Then eat quickly.

(holds the bowl. Waits. The candle gutters)

You decline.

MESSENGER

(looks at the bowl, not her)

The barley, Your Majesty. It has—
There is a mark on the rim. A—

QUEEN MARGARET

Stir the mark away.

MESSENGER

It is not—

QUEEN MARGARET

A flaw in the casting. The smith was executed. I have his bill of sale.

(holds the spoon out)

He charged too little. That was his crime. Undervaluing the metal.

MESSENGER

(the bowl in his hands now)

Your Majesty. I was sent to—

QUEEN MARGARET

Sent. Yes. By whom?

MESSENGER

The matter is—

QUEEN MARGARET

The eastern road has three commanders. Fennel. Who trembles. Bowes. Who flatters. And my husband, who—

(slips the ring off again)

Who writes单日. I mean—he writes rarely.

MESSENGER

Lord Fennel.

QUEEN MARGARET

Fennel. Poor Fennel.

(takes the bowl back. Sets it down)

He would not send someone to stand so long in a courtyard. Fennel would rush you in. Rush the words out. Fennel is afraid of silence. What a man fears—

(retrieves the candle. Drips wax onto the table)

—he assumes a woman shares.

MESSENGER

Lord Fennel is dead, Your Majesty.

(Silence. She shapes the cooling wax with her thumb.)

QUEEN MARGARET

This candle. They brought forty from the south coast. Forty for my fortieth summer. I burned them at feasts. Sixteen remain. I count now.

(another drop)

You will need another ring.

MESSENGER

Your Majesty?

QUEEN MARGARET

For the seal. You carry dispatches. The seal wears the ring. My husband's ring. Fennel would have sent his own. Fennel was never—

(shapes the wax again)

But you said Fennel is dead.

MESSENGER

At the crossing. Before dawn.

QUEEN MARGARET

And Bowes?

MESSENGER

Lord Bowes—

QUEEN MARGARET

No. The flatterer would send a flourish. A speech. Not you. You have the look of someone who rehearses—

(places the ring flat on the table)

—and rehearses—and finds each version wanting.

(pushes the ring toward him)

My husband is not dead.

MESSENGER

Your Majesty, I am not—

QUEEN MARGARET

You are not permitted to kill kings. That is the point of messengers. Permission withheld in advance. The uniform protects. The office absolves.

MESSENGER

The king—

QUEEN MARGARET

A wound then. A fever. A horse that—

MESSENGER

The king led the charge at—

QUEEN MARGARET

At. Yes. At something.

(picks up the ring. It will not fit her thumb)

He swore he would not. He swore to me. In this room. This candle was lit. We were—

She fed six children. I told you.

MESSENGER

The charge broke their center. They scattered. The king—

QUEEN MARGARET

(twisting the ring)

I wore this when we married. Then I grew. The bone—

MESSENGER

—was unhorsed.

(Silence.)

QUEEN MARGARET

Unhorsed. That is a word for letters.

MESSENGER

I was not—

QUEEN MARGARET

Sent for letters. No. You were sent for this.

(slips the ring onto the messenger's finger. It fits)

The smith. The one who—
He charged too little. But he made it fit any hand.

MESSENGER

(pulling back)

Your Majesty, the king lives. I must tell you—

QUEEN MARGARET

You are telling me.

MESSENGER

—he was taken.

QUEEN MARGARET

By whom?

MESSENGER

Ransom. They will—

QUEEN MARGARET

(holds up the bowl)

The barley. You never tasted it.

MESSENGER

—they will want—

QUEEN MARGARET

What will they want?

MESSENGER

—they will want—

QUEEN MARGARET

(shattering the bowl against the table)

What will they want!

(Silence. Pewter on stone. She breathes. He does not move.)

MESSENGER

(quietly)

The ring, Your Majesty.

QUEEN MARGARET

(holding up her empty hand)

I have no ring.

MESSENGER

It is the king's seal. For the—

QUEEN MARGARET

For the answer. Yes.

(Moves to the candle. Snuffs it.)

Darkness, messenger. Does it frighten you?

MESSENGER

(in the dark)

I cannot—

QUEEN MARGARET

What?

MESSENGER

—see to—

QUEEN MARGARET

To what?

MESSENGER

—to remove—

QUEEN MARGARET

From the dark room? Or—

MESSENGER

—this ring, Your Majesty. It will not—

(Sound of pulling. Flesh against metal.)

QUEEN MARGARET

The smith made it fit any hand.

(Sound of her moving to the door. It opens. Distant torchlight.)

MESSENGER

(in the light from the doorway)

They will kill him. If there is no—

QUEEN MARGARET

You said he lives.

MESSENGER

For now.

(She does not turn.)
