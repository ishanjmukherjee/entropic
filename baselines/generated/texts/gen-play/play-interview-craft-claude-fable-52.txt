(An office. A desk, two chairs. On the desk, a cardboard box with no lid. HALE stands behind the desk. NADIA sits, coat still on.)

HALE
You found it all right.

NADIA
Third floor. They said third floor.

HALE
Who said?

NADIA
Reception.

HALE
Reception's on four.

NADIA
Then someone on four.

(He picks a mug off the desk, looks at it, puts it in the box.)

HALE
Excuse the mess. Filing.

NADIA
Of course.

HALE
People take their coats off, normally.

NADIA
Is it going to be long?

HALE
Depends who you ask.

(He sits.)

HALE
So. Regional accounts.

NADIA
Eight years at Brennan's. Before that —

HALE
I've read it. It's a very good piece of paper. Who gave you my name?

NADIA
It was on the email.

HALE
Whose email?

NADIA
I had a question about the team, actually. How many report in?

HALE
Eleven.

NADIA
Oh. I was told nine.

(Pause.)

HALE
Were you.

NADIA
I may have misread.

HALE
Nine's not a misreading. Somebody's had to sit down and arrive at nine.

NADIA
I'm good with a team in transition. At Brennan's we —

HALE
What's your start date?

NADIA
Sorry?

HALE
On the email.

NADIA
Isn't that for you to tell me?

HALE
Humour me.

NADIA
Could I have some water?

(He looks at the box. Takes the mug out. Looks into it. Sets it on the desk between them. Nobody fills it.)

HALE
There's a machine on four.

NADIA
I'm fine.

HALE
You asked.

NADIA
I'm fine now.

HALE
Tell me about a time you had to deliver difficult news.

NADIA
Is that on the form?

HALE
It's on every form.

NADIA
I'd say I believe in people hearing things from the right person.

HALE
And if the right person's on holiday?

NADIA
Then I'd wait.

HALE
How long?

NADIA
As long as it was kind.

HALE
Kind. Eight years at Brennan's and you came out kind.

NADIA
Is the role new? It was described as new.

HALE
Described.

NADIA
An expansion.

HALE
Well, look around. Does it look like we're expanding?

(She looks at the box. He puts his hand flat on its edge.)

HALE
Filing.

NADIA
You said.

HALE
Who interviewed you first?

NADIA
This is the first.

HALE
You've got a figure of nine and a coat on. This is not the first.

NADIA
There was a conversation.

HALE
Lunch?

NADIA
Coffee.

HALE
Where?

NADIA
Does it matter where?

HALE
If it was the place on the corner, it was Marcus. If it was the hotel, it was her.

NADIA
I'd like to talk about the accounts.

HALE
The hotel, then.

NADIA
The northern book is underperforming. I've got ideas about that.

HALE
I'm sure they loved them.

NADIA
I'd want to keep what works. I'd want to keep people who know where things are.

HALE
That's generous.

NADIA
It's practical.

HALE
Do I know where things are?

NADIA
I wouldn't —

HALE
Would I be one of the nine?

NADIA
Mr Hale, I came for an interview.

HALE
And how's it going?

(Silence. She unbuttons the top button of her coat. Stops.)

NADIA
They told me you'd want to meet me.

HALE
I did. I do. I asked for it. I said, send me whoever it is.

NADIA
"Whoever it is."

HALE
Whoever's being considered.

NADIA
Right.

HALE
Are you being considered?

NADIA
You'd have to tell me.

HALE
I'd have to. Yes. That's how it would work.

(He takes a stapler from the drawer, weighs it, puts it in the box.)

NADIA
That one's the company's, I think.

(He looks at her. Takes the stapler out. Places it squarely on the desk.)

HALE
You've got an eye.

NADIA
I'm sorry. I don't know why I said that.

HALE
No, it's good. It's what they'll want. Before Christmas or after?

NADIA
I can't —

HALE
Just that. Not the date. Before or after.

NADIA
I haven't signed anything.

HALE
That's not what I asked.

NADIA
It's what I can answer.

(He nods. Picks up the mug. Holds it out to her across the desk.)

HALE
This was here when I came. It was Peterson's. He left it on the desk for me, no note. I thought for a year he'd forgotten it.

(She doesn't take it.)

HALE
Go on.

(She takes it. He stands, lifts the box, tucks it under one arm, and waits. She stays in the chair, the mug in both hands, looking at the stapler.)
