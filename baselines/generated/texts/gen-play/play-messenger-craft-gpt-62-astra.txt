(The QUEEN holds a little brass bell upside down. The MESSENGER wears one boot.)

QUEEN
Did you come in like that?

MESSENGER
There was nobody on the stair.

QUEEN
There are six men on the stair.

MESSENGER
Then I came in past six men.

QUEEN
Which foot is it?

MESSENGER
You can see which foot.

QUEEN
I can see which boot. Is the other foot hurt?

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
Put it down then.

(He puts his foot down.)

MESSENGER
We lost the town.

QUEEN
Which is what you said downstairs?

MESSENGER
I asked for you downstairs.

QUEEN
And they sent you up without a boot.

MESSENGER
Would you like me to go down again?

QUEEN
Where's my son?

MESSENGER
On the east bank when I left.

QUEEN
The town's on the east bank.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
You said you lost it.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
Was he on his horse?

MESSENGER
He was standing up.

QUEEN
That's a curious answer.

MESSENGER
It's what you wanted to know.

QUEEN
You don't know me as well as that.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
Was there anyone with him?

MESSENGER
A man on either side.

QUEEN
Ours?

(The MESSENGER looks at the bell.)

QUEEN
It hasn't rung yet.

MESSENGER
I know.

QUEEN
What do they tell you happens when it rings?

MESSENGER
They shut the river gate.

QUEEN
There's a little girl comes up with hot water. That's what happens when it rings.

MESSENGER
Then have some hot water.

QUEEN
Sit down.

MESSENGER
On what?

QUEEN
You'll have to wait for them to bring you something.

MESSENGER
I'll stand.

QUEEN
These two men. Did they have hold of him?

MESSENGER
One had his arm.

QUEEN
To keep him up?

MESSENGER
You could say that.

QUEEN
I could say anything. That's why I sent somebody who could look.

MESSENGER
He had both boots on.

QUEEN
Don't do that.

MESSENGER
Good ones. You'd have known them from this far.

QUEEN
Did he give you anything for me?

MESSENGER
He gave me a woman.

QUEEN
What?

MESSENGER
By the elbow. Like this. Here, he said. And then there was another one. And then a fellow carrying his father. You should see the size of the father.

QUEEN
Where are they?

MESSENGER
Where do you think I lost the boot?

QUEEN
In the mud.

MESSENGER
On the landing. There's a gap between the boards. If you put your foot in sideways you can keep the landing up level with the boat.

QUEEN
How many boats?

MESSENGER
It was the last boat.

QUEEN
How many people?

MESSENGER
Less than there were.

QUEEN
Fewer.

MESSENGER
That's good. I'll tell them.

QUEEN
You'll tell nobody anything yet.

MESSENGER
There's a boy in my boot.

QUEEN
You said it was between the boards.

MESSENGER
It is. I got my foot out and he put his in. He asked how long. I said I'd go upstairs and ask.

QUEEN
And all the time you're up here being clever—

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
You should have said that first.

MESSENGER
I asked for you downstairs.

QUEEN
Did my son get on that boat?

MESSENGER
He was sending people down.

QUEEN
After the woman. After the big man. There must have been a moment when he could—

MESSENGER
There was a moment.

QUEEN
Yes?

MESSENGER
I got on.

(The QUEEN turns the bell upright. He catches the clapper.)

QUEEN
That's my hand you're touching.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
If I call down, they'll come.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
You wouldn't get as far as the stair.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
Let go and ask me properly.

MESSENGER
I don't know the proper way.

QUEEN
Nobody seems to, this morning.

MESSENGER
Would you come down?

QUEEN
In these?

MESSENGER
You could go as far as the turn. They can see you from the gate if you stand on the turn.

QUEEN
And what will they see?

MESSENGER
That you're not up here ringing a bell.

QUEEN
And what will I see?

(He lets go.)

QUEEN
No. Put your hand back.
