MARCUS enters the vestry. He stops.

SIMON is at a small table, turning a pocket watch over in his hands.

MARCUS
No sign outside. Thought it was tomorrow.

SIMON
Ten fifteen today.

MARCUS
I was told—

SIMON
By who?

MARCUS
Doesn't matter now.

(Silence. SIMON closes the watch, opens it.)

SIMON
Rail said you'd be late.

MARCUS
You spoke?

SIMON
I informed him.

MARCUS
That was kind.

SIMON
It was practical.

(He holds up the watch.)

You recognise it.

MARCUS
Should I?

SIMON
He wore it. Sundays.

MARCUS
Did he?

SIMON
You're saying no.

MARCUS
I'm saying did he. Different words.

(MARCUS moves toward a chair, doesn't sit.)

Hell of a turnout.

SIMON
Councilmen. Two MPs.

MARCUS
He'd have enjoyed that.

SIMON
He'd have expected it.

(The watch again. Click. Close. Click. Open.)

He left it to me. Specifically.

MARCUS
He had little enough.

SIMON
This. The cufflinks you wore. The house.

MARCUS
I own no house.

SIMON
I meant my house. Where he died.

MARCUS
Ah.

SIMON
You weren't there.

MARCUS
I was informed.

SIMON
Practical again.

(SIMON sets the watch on the table. MARCUS looks at it.)

I offered to bring him. London. The specialist you named.

MARCUS
I didn't name him.

SIMON
You suggested. In your letter.

MARCUS
That was—

SIMON
Winter. Yes.

MARCUS
—different circumstances.

(SIMON laughs, once.)

You find that funny.

SIMON
I find it a sentence.

(SIMON pockets the watch. MARCUS's hand moves, stops.)

He spoke of you. The end.

MARCUS
What end?

SIMON
Hospital. Three days.

MARCUS
There was time, then.

SIMON
There was.

(MARCUS walks to the window, back.)

MARCUS
What did he say?

SIMON
That you were busy.

MARCUS
Busy.

SIMON
Your words. From the phone.

MARCUS
I never—

SIMON
You said: "Busy month, tell him I'll ring Sunday." Four Sundays running.

MARCUS
He knew my situation.

SIMON
He knew your months.

(SIMON checks the watch against the wall clock. They do not match. He adjusts it.)

Half the people out there think you're in Canada.

MARCUS
I was.

SIMON
When?

MARCUS
Recently.

SIMON
Not when he spoke of you. Not when you missed the funeral.

MARCUS
This is the funeral.

SIMON
The first. October.

(MARCUS sits, finally.)

You didn't know.

MARCUS
I was in—

SIMON
Canada. No.

(Silence.)

What are you doing here, Marcus?

MARCUS
What is required.

SIMON
Plenty requires nothing.

MARCUS
Then why attend?

SIMON
I'll ask the questions.

MARCUS
You've always—

SIMON
Not always. Not always.

(SIMON places the watch on the table between them. Steps back.)

I don't want it. The mechanism sticks. He wound it too tight, forever, trying to—

MARCUS
Trying to what?

SIMON
—keep something moving.

(SIMON moves to the door.)

Be practical. Be informed. Wear the cufflinks, someone should.

MARCUS
He meant you to have it.

SIMON
He meant something.

(SIMON exits.)

(MARCUS alone. He picks up the watch. Holds it to his ear. Shakes it. Presses it against his forehead, eyes closed. He does not wind it.)

(Blackout.)
