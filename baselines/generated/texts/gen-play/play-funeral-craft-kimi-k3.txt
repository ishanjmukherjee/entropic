(The chapel is dim. Two chairs face a closed coffin. A single object sits on the coffin: a silver hip flask. JONAH stands near it, hands folded. ELIAS enters, carrying flowers. He sees the flask, then JONAH.)

JONAH
You came.

ELIAS
I brought these.

JONAH
Sorted.

(ELIAS places the flowers by the coffin, away from the flask.)

ELIAS
I didn’t know if you’d…
The time?

JONAH
Eleven.

ELIAS
The robes still have to be hung.

JONAH
Pete’s doing it.

ELIAS
I’ll check the windows.

JONAH
They’re closed.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
I didn’t ask for the first hymn.

JONAH
Nobody did.

ELIAS
I thought maybe you…

JONAH
What?

ELIAS
Dad liked the organ.

JONAH
He did.

(ELIAS moves toward the flask. JONAH steps half-a-step in front of it.)

ELIAS
You kept it.

JONAH
He wanted it here.

ELIAS
In the chapel.

JONAH
Next to him.

ELIAS
That’s not the same.

(A beat.)

JONAH
Sit.

ELIAS
I’m all right.

JONAH
Sit anyway.

(ELIAS sits on the far chair. JONAH sits next to the coffin. They both look away from each other.)

ELIAS
The notice had my old street.

JONAH
It did.

ELIAS
Did you write it?

JONAH
The paper wrote it.

ELIAS
You gave them the old street.

JONAH
They asked.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
I had to call twice.

JONAH
I was…

ELIAS
Busy.

JONAH
Something like that.

ELIAS
I saw the photos from the wake.

JONAH
You weren’t in them.

ELIAS
No.

(A beat.)

JONAH
You could have answered.

ELIAS
I did.

JONAH
The first call.

ELIAS
A year is a long time.

JONAH
I didn’t call a year ago.

ELIAS
You didn’t call.

JONAH
I texted.

ELIAS
Two lines.

(A beat.)

JONAH
You read them.

ELIAS
Eventually.

(ELIAS looks at the flask again. JONAH shifts his weight so the flask is closer to him.)

ELIAS
Is it his?

JONAH
Mine.

ELIAS
The silver.

JONAH
They said silver was in.

ELIAS
That’s not a word for “private”.

JONAH
Where did you get that?

(ELIAS stands and walks to the coffin, keeping his eyes off JONAH. He stops beside the flowers.)

ELIAS
He’d have wanted it plain.

JONAH
He’d have wanted last Christmas.

ELIAS
I’m talking about the flask.

JONAH
You’re talking about Christmas.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
No.

JONAH
Sure.

(ELIAS turns. He does not sit.)

ELIAS
I brought a letter.

JONAH
That’s new.

ELIAS
For the box.

JONAH
There is no box.

ELIAS
A memory box.

JONAH
He said no boxes.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
When did he say that?

JONAH
Last month.

ELIAS
Last month you still…

JONAH
I called him.

ELIAS
I meant you.

(A beat. JONAH reaches into his jacket and touches the flask, but does not lift it.)

JONAH
You can put it on top.

ELIAS
The flowers?

JONAH
The letter.

ELIAS
I’m not writing on top of flowers.

JONAH
Fine.

ELIAS
It’s not about fine.

(A beat.)

JONAH
Give it here.

ELIAS
Why?

JONAH
I’ll put it with him.

ELIAS
You will?

JONAH
If you let me.

(A beat. ELIAS looks at the flask, then at JONAH’s hand, still resting near it.)

ELIAS
Why now?

JONAH
Why not.

ELIAS
You don’t touch people’s things.

JONAH
I’m not touching yours.

ELIAS
It’s not mine.

JONAH
You brought it.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
Maybe I should read it first.

JONAH
Maybe.

ELIAS
Maybe you don’t want reflexes.

JONAH
Maybe you don’t want to read it.

(A beat. ELIAS steps closer. They are both near the coffin now, on opposite sides. The flask sits between them.)

ELIAS
I could leave it.

JONAH
You could.

ELIAS
I’m not good at that.

JONAH
I remember.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
You remember wrong.

JONAH
I remember what I saw.

ELIAS
What did you see?

JONAH
You.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
That’s not a helpful picture.

JONAH
You don’t need helpful.

(A beat. ELIAS raises the letter slightly, as if to slide it under the flowers. JONAH’s hand shoots toward it and stops.)

ELIAS
Watch it.

JONAH
I am.

ELIAS
Don’t.

JONAH
I won’t.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
I’d rather he have it.

JONAH
I’d rather you did.

ELIAS
That’s clever.

JONAH
It isn’t.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
Do you think he knew?

JONAH
Knew what.

ELIAS
Where I sent the money.

JONAH
You sent money.

ELIAS
For the mortgage.

JONAH
He had a mortgage.

ELIAS
The whole thing.

JONAH
He didn’t mention it.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
He didn’t have to say.

JONAH
No.

ELIAS
So you’ll tell them I sent it.

JONAH
I won’t.

ELIAS
You will.

JONAH
No.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
I don’t want them thinking I didn’t…

JONAH
Didn’t what.

(ELIAS looks toward the door, then down at the flask.)

ELIAS
Nothing.

JONAH
Okay.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
Did he ask about me?

JONAH
He asked.

ELIAS
What did he say?

JONAH
What do you think he said.

(A beat.)

ELIAS
Don’t answer everything with an echo.

JONAH
You came late.

ELIAS
I came when I could.

JONAH
You had time.

ELIAS
I had the wrong time.

(A beat.)

JONAH
That’s convenient.

ELIAS
It wasn’t convenient.

JONAH
It was convenient for you.
