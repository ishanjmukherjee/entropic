LINDA
Take a seat.

ARTHUR
Thank you for seeing me.

LINDA
(not sitting)
Was it raining?

ARTHUR
Sorry?

LINDA
Your umbrella. You're holding it.

ARTHUR
Oh. Yes.

(He looks for somewhere to put it. No stand. He keeps it.)

LINDA
So.

ARTHUR
I want to be direct.

LINDA
By all means.

ARTHUR
This position—

LINDA
—The one you applied for?

ARTHUR
The posting was slightly unclear.

LINDA
Was it.

ARTHUR
"Executive liaison." That's the title?

LINDA
Is there a problem with the title?

ARTHUR
No, I just—

LINDA
Because we can discuss the title.

ARTHUR
I want to discuss the role.

LINDA
Of course. Your previous employer.

ARTHUR
Left. I left. Last month.

LINDA
Voluntarily.

ARTHUR
There was—

LINDA
—A disagreement?

ARTHUR
A restructuring.

(She moves to the desk. Picks up a paperweight—glass, heavy, blue-green. Turns it in her hand.)

LINDA
This industry is all restructuring now.

ARTHUR
I have skills.

LINDA
I'm sure.

ARTHUR
Client retention. Negotiation.

LINDA
You kept your umbrella.

ARTHUR
What?

LINDA
Most people leave them. In the rack outside.

ARTHUR
There wasn't a—

LINDA
—There is. There was.

ARTHUR
I didn't see it.

LINDA
No.

(She sets the paperweight down. Pushes it slightly toward him.)

LINDA
Your references.

ARTHUR
I brought—

LINDA
—Mr. Henderson didn't return our call.

ARTHUR
He's traveling.

LINDA
Ms. Okonkwo did.

ARTHUR
Yes. She's—

LINDA
—Very detailed.

ARTHUR
We worked together four years.

LINDA
She mentioned a particular incident.

ARTHUR
There was no incident.

LINDA
A client dinner.

ARTHUR
(shifting)
That was handled.

LINDA
"Handled." Her word exactly.

ARTHUR
I don't know what you're—

LINDA
—The account. Meridian. Three million annually.

ARTHUR
They renewed.

LINDA
After?

ARTHUR
What do you want me to say?

(Silence. She taps the paperweight once.)

LINDA
This job requires discretion.

ARTHUR
I understand discretion.

LINDA
The umbrella again.

ARTHUR
I'm sorry?

LINDA
You're gripping it. Rather tightly.

(He looks at his hand. White-knuckled. Forces it to relax.)

ARTHUR
I can be an asset.

LINDA
Can you.

ARTHUR
Your company—I've followed the acquisitions. The Chen deal particularly.

LINDA
Public knowledge.

ARTHUR
The structure. The way you shielded the parent entity.

(Something changes. Almost a smile.)

LINDA
You find that interesting.

ARTHUR
I find it necessary.

LINDA
And if I asked you to do similar work?

ARTHUR
I would do it.

LINDA
Without questions.

ARTHUR
The right questions. At the right time.

(She laughs. Surprises herself.)

LINDA
Mr. Arthur.

ARTHUR
It's just Arthur.

LINDA
It says Martin on your application.

ARTHUR
Middle name. I never—

LINDA
—You want to be direct.

ARTHUR
I want to be considered.

(She picks up the paperweight again. Holds it out. He doesn't take it.)

LINDA
Heavy, isn't it.

ARTHUR
Not especially.

LINDA
Feel it.

ARTHUR
I don't—

LINDA
—Take it.

(He does. Tests the weight. Looks at her.)

LINDA
Now put it down.

ARTHUR
Where?

LINDA
Where you like.

(He places it on the corner of the desk. She moves it two inches left.)

LINDA
There.

ARTHUR
What was this interview about?

LINDA
What do you think?

ARTHUR
I think you knew before I arrived.

LINDA
Knew what?

ARTHUR
Whether you'd hire me.

LINDA
Then why continue?

ARTHUR
Because you need something too.

(The phone rings. She ignores it.)

LINDA
What do I need?

ARTHUR
The same thing I do.

LINDA
Which is?

ARTHUR
Someone who won't explain.

(She reaches past him. Picks up the umbrella from where he leaned it. Opens it slightly, checks the mechanism, closes it.)

LINDA
Cheap.

ARTHUR
It works.

LINDA
For now.

ARTHUR
Until it doesn't.

(She hands it back. Handle first.)

LINDA
The position starts Monday.

ARTHUR
You're offering—

LINDA
—I'm acknowledging.

ARTHUR
I don't understand.

LINDA
Yes you do.

(She opens the door. Stays on the threshold.)

ARTHUR
The paperweight.

LINDA
What about it.

ARTHUR
You wanted me to touch it.

LINDA
Did I.

ARTHUR
My fingerprints.

LINDA
Monday. Nine o'clock.

(She exits. He stands alone. Looks at the paperweight. Picks it up. Turns it once in his hand. Puts it in his coat pocket. Stands still. Listens. Opens the umbrella, slowly, fully, overhead. The fluorescent light changes, blue-green, through the fabric. Holds it. Blackout.)
