(A desk. Two chairs. MARA sits. ELLIS enters. On the desk: a brass letter opener.)

ELLIS
Thanks for seeing me.

MARA
Sit.

(He sits. She doesn't look up.)

MARA
You know the role.

ELLIS
I think so.

MARA
You think so.

ELLIS
I've read the description. I've done this kind of—
I mean. Yes.

MARA
Good.

(She turns a page. Long silence.)

MARA
Who told you about us?

ELLIS
A friend.

MARA
Which friend?

ELLIS
Someone I used to work with.

MARA
A name, Mr. Ellis.

ELLIS
Does it matter?

MARA
I'm asking you.

ELLIS
Daniel. Daniel Reyes.

MARA
I don't know a Daniel Reyes.

ELLIS
He said you did.

(She closes the folder.)

MARA
Tell me about the gap.

ELLIS
There's no gap.

MARA
March to November.

ELLIS
That's eight months. I was—
I was consulting.

MARA
Consulting in what?

ELLIS
The same field.

MARA
Which is?

ELLIS
You have my CV.

MARA
I want to hear you say it.

ELLIS
I don't understand the question.

(She picks up the letter opener. Turns it.)

MARA
You were in the building, weren't you. Tuesday.

ELLIS
What building?

MARA
This building.

ELLIS
No.

(She sets the letter opener down. Points it toward him.)

MARA
Why do you want this job?

ELLIS
I need the money.

MARA
Everyone needs money. That's not an answer.

ELLIS
It's the only answer I have.

MARA
Try again.

ELLIS
I admire the company.

MARA
The company makes packaging.

ELLIS
Steady work then. That's not a crime.

MARA
No one said it was.

(She opens the folder again. Reads.)

MARA
You were a supervisor. Two years.

ELLIS
Yes.

MARA
And now you're applying to file invoices.

ELLIS
It's a good company.

MARA
You just told me it makes packaging.

ELLIS
I meant—
What's this really about?

MARA
This is an interview, Mr. Ellis.

ELLIS
Is the job even real?

(She looks at him for the first time. Fully.)

MARA
Do you always ask that?

ELLIS
When I'm being tested, yes.

MARA
You think you're being tested.

ELLIS
I think something else is happening here.

MARA
Like what?

ELLIS
You tell me.

(She smiles. Small.)

MARA
How are you with confidentiality?

ELLIS
Fine.

MARA
Fine.

ELLIS
I'm discreet, if that's what you're—
I don't repeat things I hear.

MARA
What kinds of things?

ELLIS
Any kinds.

MARA
But you've heard things. Before. At your last—
consulting work.

ELLIS
I didn't say that.

MARA
No. You didn't.

(She takes the letter opener. Stands. Walks to the window.)

MARA
This was my father's.

ELLIS
It's nice.

MARA
He used to say a sharp tool is an honest one.

ELLIS
What does that mean?

MARA
He never explained it either.

(She sets it on the windowsill. Stays there, back to him.)

MARA
Daniel Reyes. Describe him.

ELLIS
Why?

MARA
Humor me.

ELLIS
Tall. Glasses. Left us in the spring.

MARA
Left.

ELLIS
Or was let go. He never said which.

MARA
You never asked.

ELLIS
You learn not to.

MARA
Where did you learn that?

ELLIS
Places like this.

(She turns.)

MARA
You're not going to get the filing job.

ELLIS
Is there a different one?

MARA
There might be.

ELLIS
Doing what?

MARA
Sitting where I'm sitting. Someday. Asking questions like mine.

ELLIS
Why me?

MARA
Because you lied about Tuesday.

ELLIS
I didn't.

MARA
Then because you lied well enough that I had to check.

(She sits again. Slides the folder toward him.)

MARA
Sign the last page.

ELLIS
What is it?

MARA
A start.

(He looks at the folder. Looks at her.)

ELLIS
And Daniel?

MARA
There's no Daniel, Mr. Ellis.

ELLIS
You asked me to describe him.

MARA
I did.

(He reaches for the pen. Stops.)

ELLIS
What happened on Tuesday?

(She picks up the letter opener from the sill. Sets it back on the desk. Between them.)

MARA
Sign.

(He picks up the pen. Doesn't sign. Holds it.)

MARA
Well?

(He looks at the letter opener. She watches him look at it.)

ELLIS
You first.

(He sets the pen down. She reaches—)
