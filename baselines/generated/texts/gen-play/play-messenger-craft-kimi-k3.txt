(A chair, a table. On the table, a metal cup of wine. QUEEN stands by the chair, not sitting. MESSENGER remains near the door, cloak wet, mud to the knee.)

MESSENGER
You sent for me, Majesty.

QUEEN
The gate sent for you. I asked for the count.

MESSENGER
The road count, or the river count?

QUEEN
Sit.

MESSENGER
I've been sitting on a horse for nine days.

QUEEN
Then standing is a relief.

MESSENGER
Your kindness—

QUEEN
Don't start thanking me yet. Have you eaten?

MESSENGER
On the road.

QUEEN
There's wine.

MESSENGER
I know. I can see it.

QUEEN
Then you know I had it poured an hour ago. When I heard the bridge.

MESSENGER
The bridge held.

QUEEN
Fairfield burned.

MESSENGER
The mill. Not the town.

QUEEN
You say that quickly.

MESSENGER
I've practiced it. It was the first thing every village asked.

QUEEN
And the Duke?

MESSENGER
They asked me that too.

QUEEN
I ask it slower.

MESSENGER
When I left, he was at the ridge. Planning to engage at dawn.

QUEEN
When you left.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
Which was?

MESSENGER
Three days. Four, counting the—

QUEEN
Don't count it wrong to make it longer.

MESSENGER
Three days, then.

QUEEN
And in three days, a plan can change.

MESSENGER
Plans do.

QUEEN
But you brought no letter.

MESSENGER
The last courier took the letters.

QUEEN
The last courier. So you've spoken to him.

MESSENGER
I passed him on the road. He was riding north.

QUEEN
Riding. Alive.

MESSENGER
When I passed him.

QUEEN
With letters from the Duke.

MESSENGER
He didn't stop to show me his bag.

QUEEN
Did you stop?

MESSENGER
I had instruction not to stop for anything.

QUEEN
Even other couriers. Even if they were riding from the front. Even if—

MESSENGER
Even then.

QUEEN
(She looks at the cup.) It's gone cold.

MESSENGER
Wine keeps.

QUEEN
Metal cup. It takes the cold. I had them use the metal. So you would know I wasn't afraid of poison.

MESSENGER
I would never think that.

QUEEN
You thought it. On the road. You thought: if it's bad news, she may blame the cup. She may blame the bringer.

MESSENGER
I thought about the mud.

QUEEN
The Duke's signet. Do you wear it?

MESSENGER
I'm not noble, Majesty.

QUEEN
Then turn out your sleeve.

MESSENGER
Why?

QUEEN
You favor the left arm. When you take off your cloak, you hold it with your teeth. When you bow, you keep the left hand in.

MESSENGER
Old wound. Before the war.

QUEEN
Show me.

MESSENGER
It's nothing to show a queen.

QUEEN
I've seen men's arms before.

MESSENGER
Not this one.

QUEEN
(She steps closer.) Is the ridge where you left him?

MESSENGER
Majesty—

QUEEN
Is the Duke at the ridge?

MESSENGER
He was.

QUEEN
When you left.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
And the courier you passed. He had been at the ridge too?

MESSENGER
I didn't ask him where he'd been.

QUEEN
But you smelled the smoke.

MESSENGER
Everyone smells of smoke on that road.

QUEEN
Not pine smoke. Not the mill.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
Oak. The ridge is oak.

MESSENGER
I don't know about trees.

QUEEN
You know about horses. The Duke's horse. White sock on the near fore.

MESSENGER
Many horses—

QUEEN
Did you see it?

MESSENGER
Majesty, let me deliver my message.

QUEEN
I thought you had. You said the bridge held. You said the mill burned. You said the Duke was planning. That was your message.

MESSENGER
That was the road.

QUEEN
Then give me the rest.

MESSENGER
There is no rest.

QUEEN
Your sleeve says there is.

MESSENGER
(He pulls off his left glove. The arm is bound in linen, dark through.) The horse took me down at the river. I walked the last mile.

QUEEN
And the Duke's horse?

MESSENGER
I don't know.

QUEEN
You do.

MESSENGER
I know the courier told me to tell you one thing. He said to say: the wine at the high table is sweet this year.

QUEEN
He said that.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
And you believed it meant something.

MESSENGER
I believed it was cheaper than a letter.

QUEEN
(She picks up the cup. She smells it.) Not sweet now.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
(She holds it out.) Drink it anyway.

MESSENGER
Majesty—

QUEEN
One of us should have something go down easy today.

MESSENGER
(He takes the cup. He looks at the wine. He puts it back on the table.) It stains the silver.

QUEEN
It does.

MESSENGER
Then better not to touch it.

QUEEN
(She turns away. She does not sit.) The ridge.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
If the Duke fell there, the line falls.

MESSENGER
It bends. Depends who commands.

QUEEN
You know who commands. The same person who always commanded the retreat.

MESSENGER
I only carry words.

QUEEN
Carry one more, then. Go to the chapel. Tell the sisters to ring the bell for Fairfield mill.

MESSENGER
Just the mill?

QUEEN
Just the mill.

MESSENGER
And if they ask about the ridge?

QUEEN
Then you tell them what you told me. That you were riding from the river, and you didn't look back.

MESSENGER
(He looks at the cup. He looks at her.) You wanted the letter.

QUEEN
I wanted to know if I should sit while you spoke. Now I know I shouldn't.

MESSENGER
(He puts his glove back on.) The courier—

QUEEN
Go.

MESSENGER
He had your ring. The one with the black stone. He said to keep it safe until I—

QUEEN
You didn't pass him on the road.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
(She finally sits. She takes the cup, holds it, does not drink.) You came from the ridge.

MESSENGER
I came from the Duke.

QUEEN
(She sets the cup down.) Tell the sisters to ring for Fairfield.
