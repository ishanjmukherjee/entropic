ALEX

BEN
(Through his teeth)

ALEX

ALEX
No.

BEN

ALEX

(Beat. BEN twists his wrist free. He takes a step back. He places the hip flask, open, on top of the headstone. The whiskey fumes rise between them.)

BEN
Fine.

(He turns and walks three paces down the aisle. He stops.)

BEN
(His back to ALEX)
I needed the money. I know he told you. That’s why I stopped calling. I couldn’t ask again and I couldn’t talk about the humidity anymore. So. Now you know. I needed the money and I was ashamed.

ALEX
The will’s been read. The house is yours. He left it to you. All of it.
