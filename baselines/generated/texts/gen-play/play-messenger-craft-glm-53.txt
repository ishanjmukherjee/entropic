(An antechamber. Evening. A large map of the war country spread on a table. QUEEN stands over it, moving small wooden markers with one finger. A door opens. CAPTAIN enters, muddy, one arm bandaged. He stops.)

QUEEN
You took the road through the pass.

CAPTAIN
The road was held, Majesty.

QUEEN
By whom?

CAPTAIN
May I put this down?

(He indicates a dispatch tube. She takes it from him without opening it and sets it on the map, on the eastern towns.)

QUEEN
You came fast.

CAPTAIN
Horses died under me.

QUEEN
Good horses?

CAPTAIN
The best I had.

QUEEN
Then the news is worth three horses.

CAPTAIN
Worth more.

QUEEN
(stepping back from the map)
Say it standing there.

CAPTAIN
Majesty—

QUEEN
There. Where I can see all of you.

(He stands where she stood. He looks at the map. She looks at him.)

CAPTAIN
The pass is taken. Lord Arenhold is—

QUEEN
Is.

CAPTAIN
Unaccounted for.

QUEEN
Dead men are accounted for. Dead men are the easiest thing in the world to account for. Unaccounted for means what?

CAPTAIN
It means no body.

QUEEN
It means he ran.

CAPTAIN
It means no body, Majesty.

QUEEN
Pick up the marker at the pass.

(He hesitates. He picks up the small wooden marker.)

QUEEN
Now set it where you know it belongs.

CAPTAIN
I do not know where it belongs.

QUEEN
You have been on that field.

CAPTAIN
I have.

QUEEN
You saw what you saw. Set it where you saw it.

(He sets it down, gently, well behind the pass. South. She comes to the table and looks at where he put it. She does not touch it.)

QUEEN
The river towns.

CAPTAIN
Yes.

QUEEN
And the river itself?

CAPTAIN
Still ours.

QUEEN
Still ours.

(She moves two markers from the north down to the river. Her hand is steady. She does not look at him.)

CAPTAIN
Majesty, there is more in the tube.

QUEEN
I know what is in the tube.

CAPTAIN
You have not opened it.

QUEEN
I do not need to open it. It is thin. Bad news from Arenhold is always thin. When he wins he fills four pages. When he loses, one line. Everyone knows this about him.

(She opens the tube. One line. She reads it. She folds it once and puts it in her sleeve.)

QUEEN
The boy?

CAPTAIN
Which boy, Majesty?

QUEEN
There is one boy.

CAPTAIN
(Slowly)
The King your son was with the baggage. The baggage is intact.

QUEEN
Intact.

CAPTAIN
Guarded. Moving south under escort.

QUEEN
Who chose the south?

CAPTAIN
The Prince chose the south.

QUEEN
He chose. Twelve years old, and he chose.

CAPTAIN
He chose well, Majesty. The baggage lives because of it.

QUEEN
(quiet)
Do not tell me he chose well. Do not stand there muddy and honest and tell me a child chose well in a field I sent men to hold.

CAPTAIN
I tell you what I saw.

QUEEN
You tell me what you saw.

(She turns back to the map. She hovers her hand over the marker he placed. She does not move it.)

CAPTAIN
Majesty. Orders.

QUEEN
For what?

CAPTAIN
The army. Ten thousand men on the river are waiting on a word.

QUEEN
They can wait ten minutes more. Ten thousand men waiting are ten thousand men not dying.

CAPTAIN
Majesty—

QUEEN
Have you eaten?

CAPTAIN
That is not—

QUEEN
It is a simple question. Have you eaten?

CAPTAIN
No.

QUEEN
You will eat. The kitchen will give you anything. Then you will sleep in the west room, the good bed, and in the morning you will tell me how the pass may be retaken, and you will not mention Arenhold's name in my hearing again this year.

CAPTAIN
And tonight?

QUEEN
Tonight the ten thousand wait.

CAPTAIN
They wait on your word, not the night's.

QUEEN
(Taking the folded paper from her sleeve. Holding it.)
Leave me.

CAPTAIN
Majesty, hour by hour the river—

QUEEN
Leave me the room.

(He goes to the door. He stops.)

CAPTAIN
Majesty. Your son asked me to give you a thing.

QUEEN
He asked you.

CAPTAIN
He cut it from the baggage before he ordered it moved. He said: she will want proof the baggage is intact.

(He takes something from his coat: a small carved wooden horse, child's work, worn smooth. He sets it on the map, by the river marker. He goes. She is alone. She picks up the horse. She holds it against her collarbone and looks at the map. With her other hand, slowly, she pushes the marker at the river north again. Then stops, hand on it, neither north nor south. Lights down.)
