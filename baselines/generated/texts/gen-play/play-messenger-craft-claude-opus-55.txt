(A small room. A table. On it, a jug and one cup. The QUEEN stands. The MESSENGER is just inside the door, mud to the thigh.)

QUEEN
You came by the river road.

MESSENGER
Madam.

QUEEN
It's flooded.

MESSENGER
It was.

(She pours water into the cup. She does not offer it.)

QUEEN
How many came back with you?

MESSENGER
I came quickest.

QUEEN
That isn't a number.

MESSENGER
The others had horses that were tired.

QUEEN
And yours wasn't.

MESSENGER
Mine died at the ford, madam. I came on foot.

QUEEN
Then you're very quick.

(Pause. He looks at the cup.)

MESSENGER
The Duke of Arle sends —

QUEEN
I didn't send for the Duke of Arle.

MESSENGER
No.

QUEEN
Did he send you?

MESSENGER
He was near me when —

QUEEN
When.

MESSENGER
When I was sent.

(She picks up the cup. Holds it.)

QUEEN
Who was near my husband?

MESSENGER
A great many men, madam.

QUEEN
Were you?

MESSENGER
In the morning.

QUEEN
And in the afternoon?

MESSENGER
The ground was very bad. You couldn't see the standard for the smoke. Then you could.

QUEEN
Then you could.

MESSENGER
It was standing.

QUEEN
Who was holding it?

MESSENGER
I couldn't say.

QUEEN
Couldn't.

MESSENGER
It was standing, madam. I'd swear to that.

QUEEN
I haven't asked you to swear.

(She sets the cup down on the far edge of the table, nearer him. He doesn't move.)

QUEEN
Sit.

MESSENGER
I'm filthy.

QUEEN
It's a chair.

(He sits on the very edge.)

MESSENGER
The bridge at Sallow is held.

QUEEN
By whom?

MESSENGER
It's held. The Duke thought you'd want to know it.

QUEEN
I want to know what I asked.

MESSENGER
They'll be four days at the least. If the rain keeps up, six.

QUEEN
Who will?

(Silence.)

MESSENGER
There's a man in the lower town has boats.

QUEEN
I know what's in the lower town.

MESSENGER
I only meant —

QUEEN
You meant boats.

MESSENGER
The Duke said —

QUEEN
You keep giving me the Duke.

MESSENGER
He's the one who's coming.

(Pause.)

QUEEN
Coming here.

MESSENGER
With what's left. To hold the walls. He said you'd want him to hold the walls.

QUEEN
Did he say I'd want it, or that I would have it?

MESSENGER
I'm not clever, madam.

QUEEN
No. You're quick.

(She comes round the table. Stands over him. Looks at his hands.)

QUEEN
Your hands are clean.

MESSENGER
I washed them at the ford.

QUEEN
After your horse died.

MESSENGER
Before.

QUEEN
You stopped to wash your hands, and then your horse died.

MESSENGER
It was the ford killed him. Not the stopping.

(She picks up his hand. Turns it over. He lets her.)

QUEEN
Did he say anything?

MESSENGER
The Duke?

QUEEN
You know who.

MESSENGER
There was a great deal of noise.

QUEEN
That isn't an answer.

MESSENGER
He said keep the line. He said it more than once.

QUEEN
Before or after.

MESSENGER
After what, madam?

(She drops his hand.)

QUEEN
Drink your water.

MESSENGER
I'm not thirsty.

QUEEN
You've walked from the ford.

MESSENGER
I drank at the ford.

QUEEN
While you were washing.

MESSENGER
Yes.

QUEEN
You did a great many things at that ford.

(He stands.)

MESSENGER
The Duke asks — he'd want to know what I'm to tell him. When he's at the gate.

QUEEN
You're going back out?

MESSENGER
If you send me.

QUEEN
And if I don't?

MESSENGER
Then I'd stay, madam. I'd be glad to stay.

QUEEN
I'm sure.

MESSENGER
I'd be useful.

QUEEN
To whom?

(Pause.)

MESSENGER
He kept the line. I'd tell anyone that. I'd tell it in the square. He kept it right to the end.

QUEEN
The end of what?

MESSENGER
The day, madam.

QUEEN
And you'd tell it in the square.

MESSENGER
If it was wanted.

QUEEN
Who'd want it?

MESSENGER
You would.

(Silence. She looks at him a long time.)

QUEEN
What's your name?

MESSENGER
Does it matter?

QUEEN
It will to someone.

(He doesn't answer. She picks up the cup and holds it out to him. He looks at it. He doesn't take it. She doesn't lower it.)

QUEEN
Go on.

(He reaches. Stops, his hand an inch from hers.)

MESSENGER
Which gate, madam?

(She doesn't answer. Neither of them moves.)
