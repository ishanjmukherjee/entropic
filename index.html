how to read this

Underneath, a language model is a next-word predictor trained on a large slice of the public internet. For every token of your text (a word or a piece of one), we ask how much probability the model gave to the token you actually wrote, given everything before it. That probability is turned into surprisal: 0 bits means the model was certain, each extra bit means half as likely. A token at 10 bits was a one-in-a-thousand guess.

Single tokens are noisy: any name or number is a surprise. So the page shades passages of one to three sentences by their average surprisal, which is steadier and easier to read. Hover or tap a passage to see its average and the tokens that drove it. Tick "per token" to shade every token on its own and see what the model expected at each one.

If the page is mostly green, you are restating what the model already knows, and a reader could have gotten it by asking one. Red is where you are adding something: a claim, a detail, a turn of phrase it had no way to anticipate.

Caveats worth keeping in mind. Names, numbers, and typos are surprising without being insightful, so read red as "information", not "quality". Famous passages the model has memorized come out green even though a person wrote them. Later parts of a text are judged knowing the earlier parts, so a term you coined turns green once you have used it a few times. And the score depends on the model: this one is , given your text with no instructions and no chat formatting, so it acts as a plain next-word predictor. That setup was chosen by a preregistered test on recent human writing no model had seen.

The idea follows Gwern's advice on writing for LLMs: the text worth publishing is the text a model could not have produced on its own.

settings and privacy

Your text is sent from your browser straight to Featherless for scoring and is not stored by this site. There is no server behind this page.

Scoring runs on a small shared budget, which also caps each text at about 10,000 words. If the budget runs dry, or you want longer texts, paste your own Featherless API key below. It is kept only in this browser.
